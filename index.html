FAQ

Common questions.

How can a creator join VCS?

Open the Creator Portal, register an account, verify it, complete the roster application and wait for admin review.

Does every approved creator appear publicly?

No. VCS can approve a creator internally while keeping the public roster toggle off. Public visibility is a separate admin decision.

How are campaign forms added?

Admin creates the campaign brief and manually pastes a Google Form URL. The creator sees that URL only on an active campaign. Campaign application links are controlled by VCS Admin.

Can brand logos and digital pages be added later?

Yes. Both are managed from the admin panel and appear on the public website automatically.

How does Instagram verification work?

Production uses Meta's official Instagram API for eligible professional Creator/Business accounts. Personal or unsupported accounts can still apply but remain marked for VCS manual verification.

Why do clients enter details before downloading the portfolio?

VCS uses the lead gate to record who downloaded the currently published portfolio version and what service they are interested in. The details appear in Admin → Leads.
